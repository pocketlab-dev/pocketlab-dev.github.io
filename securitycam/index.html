SecurityCam

A personal Android home security camera app for one household.

SecurityCam turns an old Android phone into a home security camera. When it detects motion or sound, it records a short clip, encrypts it on the phone (AES-256-GCM) and delivers it to the owner's own viewer phone. The app is not published in any app store and is used only by its developer.
