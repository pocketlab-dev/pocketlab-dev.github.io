SecurityCam privacy policy

What the app accesses

With the user's permission, the camera app uses the Google Drive scope drive.file. It can only see files it created itself, never the rest of the user's Drive.

How data is used

Video clips are encrypted on the phone before upload, stored temporarily in the user's own Google Drive, downloaded by the user's own viewer app and then deleted. The developer runs no server and receives no data.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

Disconnecting Google Drive in the app deletes its clips from Drive and revokes its access. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
